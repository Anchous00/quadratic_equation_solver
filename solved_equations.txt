Equation 1: x^2-5x+6 = 0
Solution:
coefficients: a = 1, b = -5, c = 6
Discriminant D = b * b - 4 * a * c = -5 * -5 - 4 * 1 * 6 = 1
D > 0, two roots:
x1 = (-b + sqrt(D))/(2 * a) = (5 + sqrt(1))/(2 * 1)
x2 = (-b - sqrt(D))/(2 * a) = (5 - sqrt(1))/(2 * 1)
Roots: 3, 2
